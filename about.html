PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

Dr. A. T. Ahamed Mohideen

A qualified and dedicated homoeopathic physician, researcher, and paediatric specialist combining clinical experience, academic research, and medical education.

WHO WE ARE & BACKGROUND

A Multidisciplinary Medical Approach

Dr. A. T. Ahamed Mohideen completed his Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery (B.H.M.S.) from Venkateswara Homoeopathic Medical College & Hospital, Porur, Chennai, followed by postgraduate specialization — Doctor of Medicine (M.D.) in Homoeopathy in Paediatrics — from Sarada Krishna Homoeopathic Medical College, Kanniyakumari.

His professional journey encompasses clinical consultation, public health initiatives, academic teaching in Community Medicine, and government-funded medical research. He currently serves as Senior Project Associate in the Division of Medical Research at SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST), Kattankulathur.

Dr. Mohideen's clinical work emphasizes individual case analysis, thorough clinical examination, and compassionate paediatric care, while maintaining active involvement in evidence-based research initiatives.
